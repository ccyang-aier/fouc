Chat emoji artwork

Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D artwork, Copyright Microsoft Corporation, is used under the MIT license. The default artwork is resized and encoded as WebP. Source: https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji

Missing Unicode 17 and skin-tone variants use Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji. The original SVG artwork is retained.

Emoji names, grouping and Chinese/English search annotations use Unicode Emoji 17 and Unicode CLDR data under the accompanying Unicode license. Source: https://github.com/unicode-org/cldr-json

Exact revisions and conversion settings are recorded in sources.json. Rebuild with `node scripts/build-chat-emoji-assets.mjs`; no CDN is contacted by the application at runtime.
